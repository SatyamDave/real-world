# Governing Public World Simulators

- **Claim:** A public world simulator should be governed as a forecasting instrument under public scoring, with structural limits in its schema and API (populations not persons, shocks not messages and no political persuasion testing, labels in the output, a typed uncertainty statement on every number and a track record shown with its size, fail-closed checks), each paired with an audit or conformance check that is pre-registered before it runs and states its failure consequence, the release stage it gates, and the changes that void a pass. The rules are proposals; the company has not adopted them.
- **Stage:** internal review
- **Target venue:** ACM FAccT (alternate: AAAI/ACM AIES)
- **Length:** 49 pages in the house template (11pt article, single column): about 28 pages of main text, 11 of references, and a 10-page appendix holding the full audit protocols. Confirm the main-text limit in the current FAccT call before submission; the appendix is where protocol detail goes if the main text must shrink.
