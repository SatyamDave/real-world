# Belief Assimilation

- **Claim:** A two-level ensemble filter (local ETKF over segment-level belief moments and parameters, observation operators evaluated on full agent populations, and a loading-weighted lift back to agents) can correct a 100,000-agent anchored opinion-dynamics population, seeded and forced by LLMs, against polls, markets and search without adding spurious or erasing real within-segment heterogeneity. The propositions say when the lift matches the exact per-agent Kalman update and how much noise a finite-ensemble per-agent update adds. Whether the filter beats a free run, poll aggregators, a segment-only model or the market is a pre-specified hypothesis, not a result.
- **Stage:** internal review
- **Target venue:** a registered-report or workshop track first (the main-track reviewers' consensus is that a methods paper with no experiments is not competitive there); main track once E1 and E2 results exist. arXiv preprint first.
- **Build:** `cd papers/belief-assimilation && tectonic paper.tex` (compiles with no warnings and no undefined citations).
