# real-world-bench

- **Claim:** Population simulators and forecasters should be judged only on predictions committed before the answer existed, against cheap baselines (last result, single prompt on the frontier model and on the simulator's own backbone, same-feed LLM, market price); the Real World Benchmark pre-registers three such tracks (recurring polls with subgroups committed before fieldwork, rule-generated calls against markets, pricing-experiment outcomes) with the rules and tests to run them.
- **Stage:** internal review (pre-registration; no system scored yet)
- **Target venue:** NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks Track
- **Build:** `cd papers/real-world-bench && tectonic paper.tex` compiles with no errors, no undefined citations or references and no overfull boxes (2026-10-11, after the second review revision).

## Review log (2026-10-11, first revision)

Three internal reviews (methods/statistics, citation audit, adversarial reviewer). Fixed:

- **Track A live sources:** Pew spring 2026 is now development-only (ineligible for any entry with an information cutoff on or after 8 Feb 2026). Live items are trend items from named recurring series (Pew spring survey, Ipsos What Worries the World), committed before fieldwork can start (Pew 2027: 1 Jan 2027); void rules for changed or unpublished items; expected yield moved to a round-open census artifact.
- **Information cutoff:** now covers weights, retrieval, population source data, assimilation inputs and tuning data, declared in a hashed system card with code and config hashes.
- **Divisions:** organizer registers RW-blind (market feeds and market-mentioning news removed) and RW-full; all Track B hypotheses use RW-blind.
- **Commit-reveal:** operator-built tree, one fixed slot per (entry, item, horizon), leaf count published, live leaf log, unrevealed = worst outcome; market reference at provable time (superseded in the second revision: an OpenTimestamps calendar receipt carries no signed time); time-weighted 60-minute midpoint, spread <= 0.05 and volume floor, 0.10 sensitivity; hashed order-book snapshots; resolution matching and void rules.
- **Item selection:** Track B items generated by hashed code (calendar templates + every qualifying Kalshi/Polymarket contract, seeded sampling); rule-based clusters and status-quo outcomes; simultaneous public release; round 1 labelled a self-evaluation until external entrants register.
- **Scores:** Track A primary score is multiclass squared error Q (unbiased paired differences under sampling noise); n_eff from published margins of error; S kept as a descriptive ratio of sums plus a SimBench-style mean with a 2F_i exclusion; noise-corrected gap ratio kappa over disjoint same-dimension pairs, modal option defined from the human topline, sign agreement only above noise.
- **Abstention:** imputation is the worse of uniform and last result (A) or running prior (C); 90% coverage needed to rank; argument restated in proper-score terms (works in the market-blind division).
- **Rounds and inference:** rounds, windows and analysis dates defined; one-sided confirmatory family of eight tests with one primary endpoint each, Holm; intersection of eligible items; not-tested rule; no interim intervals; bootstrap rank intervals replace transitive-tie groups; minimum counts in clusters.
- **Hypotheses:** added tests against the strongest cheap rival (H2: last wave) and the same-feed LLM (H7); H1 now against SP-backbone; null-predicting parts of old H4/H6 recast as estimation; old H6 removed and disclosed as not blind (written after an internal prototype was scored on the pilot set); ablations defined and labelled exploratory.
- **Power:** MDE table for Tracks A and B at Holm's strictest level; Prophet Arena-sized gap needs ~12,400 clusters (~8,700 unadjusted).
- **Track C:** outcome classes defined at the partner's alpha, inconclusive handled, lift buckets include negative lift, RPS scoring, balanced accuracy replaces recall-only tradeoff metric, running live prior (retro pool not used), partner pre-registration of end date and publication consent, retro pilot counts removed until imported, holdout replaced by per-entry pre/post-cutoff split.
- **Baselines:** elicitation specified (verbalized JSON, temperature 0, two answer orders averaged), T1 fixed for tests, SP-backbone and feed-only LLM added, deprecation rule, demographic-lookup fallback and licensing; drift and base-rate baselines added as exploratory.
- **Statistics citations:** Kunsch replaced by wild cluster bootstrap (Cameron, Gelbach and Miller 2008); Diebold-Mariano test dropped; uncited skill-ratio bias claim replaced by a reason that needs no citation; log-score orientation and strict propriety over [0.01, 0.99] stated; CORP reliability diagrams; numeric sub-track cut.
- **Citations:** Sainz year fixed (EMNLP Findings 2023, Anthology DOI); Brier URL fixed; Gebru DOI added; Roberts dropped; IPUMS cited; Chen, Durmus, FutureEval (retrieval, five samples), Pew sample and Strat7 (trade press) descriptions corrected; added Ashokkumar 2026, DellaVigna 2019, Kohavi 2009 slides, Haber-Stornetta, Nosek 2018, Manski 2006, Wang 2025, Dominguez-Olmedo, Paleka, Cheng, Autocast, FRI 2026 update, Epstein RPS, Dimitriadis, Kalshi order-book endpoint, Pew datasets page, Ipsos tracker.
