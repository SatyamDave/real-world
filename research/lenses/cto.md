# CTO lens: the minimum engine that proves the claim

- Date: 2026-10-11
- Seat: CTO (architecture, build vs borrow, technical risk, team and tooling)
- Confidence: medium. The cost figures come from vendor price pages and the research authors' own arithmetic. None of them has been measured on our prompts yet, and that is the first thing to fix.

## Verdict

The plan is buildable in 22 days by a small team, but only if we stop treating "100,000 LLM agents" as the engine. The thing that proves the claim is a hashed, dated forecast ledger scored against market price and against cheap baselines. Behind it sits a population of 100k rows of numeric state, which the LLM touches only through a few thousand archetype calls a night. No published system calls an LLM once per agent per step at this scale. The systems that tried did worse on error than archetypes at a fraction of the cost ([../science/028-large-scale-llm-sims.md](../science/028-large-scale-llm-sims.md)). The engine is one Python package with six idempotent steps, run by cron, with all nightly LLM calls on the Batch API, Parquet snapshots in R2 and a small Postgres. No orchestrator, no streaming, no ABM framework, no social graph, no twins before launch. The two risks that can actually kill us are not engineering. The first is data licensing: IPUMS International forbids commercial use, so the census plan as written is dead. The second is the chance that a plain LLM reading the same news beats our simulation. We have to build the harness that could show that, and publish whatever it says.

## Evaluation

**What is solid.** The daily cycle maps cleanly onto a batch job. GDELT already publishes in 15-minute batches. Anthropic's Batch API holds 100,000 requests per batch at half price, and Haiku 5.5 batch is $0.05/$0.25 per million tokens. A nightly pass costs an estimated $7-28, or about $21-55 with three samples per agent ([../engineering/061-system-architecture.md](../engineering/061-system-architecture.md), [../business/089-unit-economics.md](../business/089-unit-economics.md)). Storage is negligible. Hash-and-score is about 100 lines of our own code plus a free Bitcoin-anchored timestamp ([../science/030-forecast-scoring.md](../science/030-forecast-scoring.md), [../engineering/070-commit-reveal-timestamping.md](../engineering/070-commit-reveal-timestamping.md)). Nothing here needs a GPU, Kubernetes or a vendor framework.

**What is oversold.** "100,000 agents, click any agent to ask why" implies 100,000 minds. In practice, LLM agents run independently collapse onto the modal answer: Turkish WVS concentration goes from 0.36 to 0.69. Persona richness, temperature and better models do not fix it. Real respondent data and post-hoc dispersion calibration do ([../science/018-failure-modes.md](../science/018-failure-modes.md), [../science/019-calibration-methods.md](../science/019-calibration-methods.md)). The honest engine is distribution-first. The LLM returns a verbalized answer distribution per archetype cell, agents draw from it, and a calibration map fitted on survey data corrects it. "Ask why" is a structured panel (belief deltas, the news item that moved them) plus a clearly labelled simulated narrative from a small deep-agent tier ([../engineering/065-belief-update-operators.md](../engineering/065-belief-update-operators.md), [../product/087-trust-and-explainability.md](../product/087-trust-and-explainability.md)).

**What is risky.** One: forks multiply cost. A full-population 7-day, 5-member fork costs about $350-600. One viral afternoon of uncapped free forks costs five figures ([../business/089-unit-economics.md](../business/089-unit-economics.md)). Two: a 2026 preprint found the no-persona "describe the population" call beat persona panels on 9 of 9 pairs ([../science/016-silicon-samples.md](../science/016-silicon-samples.md)). ForecastBench found LLMs mostly copy market prices ([../market/006-ai-forecasting-companies.md](../market/006-ai-forecasting-companies.md)). If our swarm adds nothing over Haiku reading the same news, we must learn that in week one, not in a Show HN thread. Three: by Oct 27 we will have a few dozen resolved questions at most, which is statistically empty. The claim at launch is "an unbackfillable record exists", not "we beat the market" ([../science/097-benchmark-design.md](../science/097-benchmark-design.md)). Four: licensing. IPUMS International bans commercial use and redistribution. WVS, Pew, Gallup and the barometers have unconfirmed commercial terms ([../data/060-data-licensing-legal.md](../data/060-data-licensing-legal.md), [../data/046-ipums-international.md](../data/046-ipums-international.md)).

**Build vs borrow.** Borrow data and ideas, not runtimes. OASIS, AgentSociety and Concordia are research frameworks built for other shapes of problem. AgentTorch and FLAME GPU are AGPL, which reaches a hosted API. Mesa adds nothing when the bottleneck is LLM calls ([../open-source/031-oasis.md](../open-source/031-oasis.md), [../open-source/033-agenttorch.md](../open-source/033-agenttorch.md), [../open-source/034-concordia.md](../open-source/034-concordia.md), [../open-source/035-classic-abm.md](../open-source/035-classic-abm.md)). What we borrow: GLOPOP-S (CC BY 4.0) as the demographic seed, DuckDB, trafilatura, react-globe.gl, the Anthropic Batch API, and DAPPER and scoringrules as test oracles only. What we write: the sampler, the update operator, assimilation, the ledger and the scorer, each a small module with one owner.

## Top 10 decisions, in priority order

1. **Hash the first forecast within 72 hours, before the engine exists.** Days elapsed is the only moat nobody can buy. Day one is a daily Merkle root over salted canonical-JSON leaves, chained to the previous root. It is stamped with OpenTimestamps and FreeTSA and mirrored to a public git repo. Each leaf freezes the question, resolution source, market price at commit, model id and config hash. The first forecasts come from the plain LLM baseline, labelled as such. ([../engineering/070-commit-reveal-timestamping.md](../engineering/070-commit-reveal-timestamping.md), [../science/030-forecast-scoring.md](../science/030-forecast-scoring.md), [../business/092-moat-theory.md](../business/092-moat-theory.md), [../product/084-launch-channels.md](../product/084-launch-channels.md))

2. **Baselines before the swarm.** Every question is scored daily for five forecasters: market price, persistence, a non-LLM demographic lookup, a plain LLM with news (5-8 samples, trimmed mean), and our simulation. The receipts page shows all five with n. If the simulation does not beat the plain LLM by Oct 27, we say so and still ship. ([../science/021-llm-forecasting.md](../science/021-llm-forecasting.md), [../science/016-silicon-samples.md](../science/016-silicon-samples.md), [../business/093-precedents-and-failures.md](../business/093-precedents-and-failures.md), [../science/098-darpa-socialsim.md](../science/098-darpa-socialsim.md))

3. **One Python package, six steps, cron.** The steps are `rw.daily ingest | assimilate | forecast | publish | score | learn --date`. Each step writes `snapshots/YYYY-MM-DD/` and skips work already done, and a `stage_runs` table stores the batch id so a crash resumes instead of paying twice. A GitHub Actions cron triggers it, with a dead-man alert if the day's root is not published by 08:00 UTC. No Dagster, Temporal, Airflow or Kafka until the run has more than 10 steps or fails weekly. ([../engineering/061-system-architecture.md](../engineering/061-system-architecture.md), [../engineering/067-orchestration.md](../engineering/067-orchestration.md))

4. **Agents are rows; the LLM is called per archetype.** 100k agents as zstd Parquet rows hold a static persona, a logit belief vector, the anchor `b0` and a stubbornness `s`. About 1,000-2,000 archetype cells get one verbalized-distribution call each night, with option order randomized and the permutation logged. Agents draw from their cell's distribution with a persistent per-agent offset. A deep tier of about 500 activation-sampled agents gets full persona prompts for "ask why". We publish how many agents were LLM-called today. ([../science/028-large-scale-llm-sims.md](../science/028-large-scale-llm-sims.md), [../engineering/063-typed-probabilities.md](../engineering/063-typed-probabilities.md), [../engineering/064-agent-state-storage.md](../engineering/064-agent-state-storage.md), [../open-source/033-agenttorch.md](../open-source/033-agenttorch.md))

5. **Numeric update, capped nudging, no EnKF yet.** The update is one `update_beliefs()`: bounded additive step, `(1-s)` susceptibility, step cap, and a pull back to `b0`. The LLM extracts a signed signal once per news cluster, never per agent, and never sets the step size. Assimilation is capped per-segment nudging in logit space, stored as an auditable field. A 20-line numpy ETKF comes in only if a 90-day hindcast shows nudging overfits. A property test checks that with zero evidence, variance does not shrink. ([../engineering/065-belief-update-operators.md](../engineering/065-belief-update-operators.md), [../science/022-data-assimilation-abm.md](../science/022-data-assimilation-abm.md), [../open-source/038-data-assimilation-libs.md](../open-source/038-data-assimilation-libs.md))

6. **A license gate in code.** `data/SOURCES.md` lists each source's license, commercial use, redistribution and citation string, and the loader refuses any source whose row says "unknown". IPUMS International is banned from anything shipped, and GADM is banned. The seed is GLOPOP-S plus WorldPop, with attitudes attached by donor matching from WVS once its terms are confirmed in writing. Until then, attitudes come from published aggregates only, and the method is labelled "calibrated to published marginals". Licensing emails go out Monday: IPUMS, WVSA, Pew, Afrobarometer, GDELT, Kalshi and Polymarket. ([../data/060-data-licensing-legal.md](../data/060-data-licensing-legal.md), [../open-source/037-synthpop-tools.md](../open-source/037-synthpop-tools.md), [../data/057-global-synthetic-population.md](../data/057-global-synthetic-population.md), [../data/058-geodata.md](../data/058-geodata.md), [../data/047-world-values-survey.md](../data/047-world-values-survey.md))

7. **A cost ledger and hard caps that fail closed.** One row per LLM call records model, input, cached and output tokens, cost in micro-dollars, and purpose. A 1,000-agent pilot in five scripts this week replaces every token estimate in the research. The nightly budget is $25 with an alarm at $60. Prompts put the shared prefix first, cap output at about 200 tokens, and stay under 100k tokens. Free forks are signed-in only, 3 a day, 200 agents and 1 member, cached by input hash. Paid forks are capped at 5,000 sampled agents, and `max_cost` is checked before any call. ([../business/089-unit-economics.md](../business/089-unit-economics.md), [../engineering/062-llm-cost-choices.md](../engineering/062-llm-cost-choices.md), [../engineering/072-replay-and-caching.md](../engineering/072-replay-and-caching.md), [../market/013-api-pricing-benchmarks.md](../market/013-api-pricing-benchmarks.md))

8. **Freeze the API contract by Oct 16.** `POST /v1/simulate` returns 202 and a job id. It takes an Idempotency-Key and a date-pinned version, with an SSE stream that resumes from Last-Event-ID. The result is defined in JSON Schema 2020-12 and every result carries `simulated: true`, `world_snapshot_id`, model, seed, ensemble spread, coverage tier, agents sampled, cost and a content hash. SDKs are generated from that schema (Stainless free tier, a one-day timebox), and CI fails on drift. Under 25 endpoints. ([../engineering/069-api-design.md](../engineering/069-api-design.md), [../open-source/045-sdk-generation.md](../open-source/045-sdk-generation.md), [../engineering/074-abuse-and-safety.md](../engineering/074-abuse-and-safety.md))

9. **Spread is a health metric, not decoration.** Every night we log the simulated-to-survey SD ratio, entropy and TV distance per country and topic. The run fails, or the country is marked "experimental", when the SD ratio leaves 0.8-1.2. Every country gets a coverage grade (A-D on the 0-100 rubric), and launch claims are made only for A/B countries. ([../science/018-failure-modes.md](../science/018-failure-modes.md), [../data/099-country-coverage-tiers.md](../data/099-country-coverage-tiers.md), [../science/027-cross-cultural-llm-bias.md](../science/027-cross-cultural-llm-bias.md), [../engineering/066-ensembles.md](../engineering/066-ensembles.md))

10. **Boring stack, four vendors, one Makefile.** The four vendors are GitHub (code, cron, CI), Cloudflare (R2, Pages for the Vite+React web app, a Worker for receipts and OG images), Neon Postgres (users, jobs, forecasts, the ledger) and one Fly machine (FastAPI plus a single fork worker reading a Postgres queue). Python uses uv, ruff, pyright and pytest; TypeScript uses pnpm and Biome. `make setup|dev|test|check` is all a PM needs. Hard-to-reverse choices (hash format, scoring rule, update operator) go in `decisions/`. Each bounded slice (ledger, scorer, sampler, API) runs as a Zeroshot change with a written acceptance check. ([../open-source/044-python-ts-monorepo-tooling.md](../open-source/044-python-ts-monorepo-tooling.md), [../engineering/068-compute-hosting.md](../engineering/068-compute-hosting.md), [../engineering/071-web-app-stack.md](../engineering/071-web-app-stack.md), [../engineering/075-repo-and-pr-practices.md](../engineering/075-repo-and-pr-practices.md))

## What to cut before Oct 27

- **Opt-in twins.** These need GDPR Art. 9 consent, deletion and an interview pipeline, and the gain over survey-conditioned agents is a few points on one US sample ([../science/017-generative-agents.md](../science/017-generative-agents.md), [../business/091-regulation.md](../business/091-regulation.md)).
- **Neighbor talk and synthetic social networks.** Ship broadcast exposure only, and add the Friedkin-Johnsen update only if a hindcast shows the broadcast-only model misses ([../science/023-opinion-dynamics.md](../science/023-opinion-dynamics.md), [../science/024-synthetic-social-networks.md](../science/024-synthetic-social-networks.md), [../science/025-news-exposure-diffusion.md](../science/025-news-exposure-diffusion.md)).
- **A paid daily human panel.** The daily question collects user answers instead. Prolific comes after launch.
- **100k clickable dots on the globe.** Show country or H3 aggregates; fetch a single agent on click ([../open-source/041-globe-visualization.md](../open-source/041-globe-visualization.md)).
- **Native-language agent prompts, fine-tuning, self-hosted models and vLLM** ([../engineering/073-multilingual-agents.md](../engineering/073-multilingual-agents.md), [../science/029-human-behavior-models.md](../science/029-human-behavior-models.md)).
- **Social-media ingest beyond GDELT and Wikipedia pageviews**, plus Google Trends scraping ([../data/055-social-media-data.md](../data/055-social-media-data.md), [../data/052-search-and-attention.md](../data/052-search-and-attention.md)).
- **Subscriptions, enterprise tiers, webhooks, MCP beyond llms.txt, and any orchestrator.**
- **Scoring counterfactual forks.** They cannot be scored, and the receipts page says so.

## The three numbers I will watch

1. **Brier difference vs the plain-LLM baseline and vs market price, with n.** If the simulation cannot beat a plain LLM reading the same news, the 100k-agent machinery is cost without value, and the product becomes the ledger plus a cheaper engine.
2. **Simulated-to-survey SD ratio per country, with a target band of 0.8-1.2.** This is the direct measure of variance collapse and Western flattening, the two failures that discredit synthetic-respondent products.
3. **Dollars per night and per fork, from the ledger.** The ceiling is $25 a night with an alarm at $60. A fork's cost is checked before any call, and the check fails closed.
