# CRO lens: who pays first, and what can sink us

- Date: 2026-10-11
- Seat: Chief Revenue Officer and Chief Risk Officer
- Inputs: all 100 notes; read in full: market/014, product/079, business/089, data/060, business/091, product/080, product/083

## Verdict

Nobody pays for "simulate the whole world," and nothing in the research shows anyone ever has (market/014-who-buys.md). The first money will come from consumer-brand and agency insights teams paying for a short pilot in which we replicate their own past studies blind, sold by the founder, with the public hashed track record as the reason they take the call. Developers paying for API credits will be the first card swipes, but the amounts will be small. Revenue before Nov 2 will be close to zero, and that is fine: the YC application needs three paid or signed design partners and a record that cannot be backfilled, not ARR. The biggest risk is not a competitor. It is that the plan as written builds a paid product on IPUMS International, which forbids commercial use and redistribution (data/046-ipums-international.md, data/060-data-licensing-legal.md). The second is an uncapped free fork going viral and costing about $11k in one day (business/089-unit-economics.md). We fix both before launch or we do not launch.

## Evaluation

**Demand is real, but it sits in market research and it is enterprise-shaped.** Simile ($2B), Aaru (under $10M ARR after two years, sold through EY and Accenture) and Electric Twin all sell to brands, agencies, financial services and consultancies. They sell message, price and product tests paid from an existing research budget (market/014-who-buys.md, market/001-aaru.md, market/002-simile.md). No vendor publishes a price except Synthetic Users ($12,500/yr floor). Synthetic research contracts run about $12.5k-$85k a year (market/003-synthetic-research-vendors.md, product/079-pricing-models.md). Policy units pay little and slowly (one GBP 200k two-year contract, not LLM-based). Game studios are a dead end (market/010-game-npc-ai.md). Funds will not buy a 22-day track record (market/015-alt-data-finance.md). That leaves one buyer worth chasing now.

**Our edge in a sale is proof, not scale.** Every competitor reports its own accuracy and none publishes a scored record. Independent tests put synthetic respondents at 12-15 points of error, with change tracked correctly 47% of the time (market/003-synthetic-research-vendors.md). An insights lead has heard "85-92% parity" from every vendor. What they have not seen is a vendor willing to replay three of their own finished studies blind and publish the miss rate. That offer is our sales motion, and it costs us under $50 in LLM spend per study (business/089-unit-economics.md).

**The margins hold only if usage is capped.** At Haiku 5.5 prices, one agent-step costs about $0.000265 standard and $0.00013 on batch. At $1 per 1,000 steps, gross margin on LLM cost is 73-87% (business/089-unit-economics.md). A full 100k-agent, 7-day fork costs about $185-540 (business/089-unit-economics.md, product/079-pricing-models.md). The nightly run costs $14-55, which is not the danger. The danger is free, uncapped, multiplied work.

**The legal exposure is concentrated and fixable.** IPUMS-I bans commercial use outright. WVS, Pew, Gallup and the barometers either do not state commercial terms or license excerpts only (data/047-world-values-survey.md, data/048-gallup-pew-global.md, data/049-regional-barometers.md). GDELT is the only data source confirmed free for commercial use, with attribution (data/050-gdelt.md). Market data terms for Kalshi and Polymarket are unread (data/051-prediction-market-apis.md). On regulation, EU AI Act Article 50 labelling is already in force for us with no grace period. GDPR special-category rules hit opt-in twins. The CFTC only matters if we touch trading (business/091-regulation.md). Election work for campaigns is the biggest reputational landmine (product/082-icp-political.md). The brand name itself is weak and not cleared (product/085-naming-trademark.md).

## Top 10 decisions and demands, in priority order

1. **No IPUMS International in anything shipped. The launch population uses GLOPOP-S plus published marginals.** GLOPOP-S is CC BY 4.0 per its Dataverse record, covers the world, and has demographics only. Email its author to confirm commercial use given the DHS and LIS inputs. Email IPUMS for written commercial terms, and log the reply in `docs/legal/`. Until those replies arrive, our method label reads "calibrated to published marginals." Do not claim "sampled from census microdata" in the YC application or on the site. (data/046-ipums-international.md, data/057-global-synthetic-population.md, open-source/037-synthpop-tools.md, data/060-data-licensing-legal.md)

2. **Create a `data/SOURCES.md` licence table that acts as a hard gate.** Each row records the source, licence, commercial use (yes, no or unknown), redistribution, citation string, who checked it and when. The nightly job refuses any source marked "unknown." Survey sources enter only as aggregate calibration targets and never as respondent rows in traces, the API or the repo. Send written requests to WVS, Pew, Afrobarometer, Arab Barometer and Latinobarómetro this week. (data/060-data-licensing-legal.md, data/049-regional-barometers.md, data/099-country-coverage-tiers.md)

3. **Every fork and API run carries a fail-closed spend cap.** Free forks require sign-in and are limited to 3 per day, 200 agents, 7 days and 1 member, cached by input hash. `POST /v1/simulate` takes `max_credits`, estimates the cost before running, and refuses when the balance is short or the quota check errors. A global daily free-tier budget trips a kill switch. Everything goes through one cost ledger, with one row per LLM call. (business/089-unit-economics.md, market/013-api-pricing-benchmarks.md, engineering/074-abuse-and-safety.md)

4. **We sell one product, a paid replication pilot for insights teams.** The pilot costs $7,500 for 6 weeks. We replay 3 of the buyer's completed studies blind and report error by segment. After that we run their live tests with ensemble spread, and the pilot converts to $2,000 a month. This beats a self-serve plan for revenue because it sits inside a budget line that already exists and puts the buyer's own data in the proof. Target: 3 signed pilots by Nov 2, from 30 conversations with brand and agency insights leads. Every call asks what the last study cost and which survey we would replace. Those answers go into the YC application. (market/014-who-buys.md, market/007-market-research-tam.md, product/080-icp-product-teams.md)

5. **Use one public price and one meter: the agent-step.** One agent-step is one agent, one simulated day and one ensemble member. The price is $1 per 1,000 steps, plus a no-card monthly free credit worth under $1 of our cost. Pro is $49 a month and includes 50,000 steps. Every response states the credit cost. There are no other tiers, no annual plans, and "Talk to us" covers anything above 1M steps a month. Meanwhile the notes use three different unit names (agent-days, agent-answers, agent-steps). We pick agent-step and delete the others. (product/079-pricing-models.md, business/089-unit-economics.md, market/013-api-pricing-benchmarks.md)

6. **The receipts page and the daily forecast stay free and are never paywalled.** The hashed record is our acquisition channel and our sales collateral, and both stop working behind a login. We never claim "beats the market" before about 100 resolved questions. Each Brier score is shown next to the market price, a persistence baseline and an LLM-only baseline, with n stated. Misses are listed. Aaru's Semafor miss and Google Flu Trends are what overclaiming costs. (science/030-forecast-scoring.md, business/093-precedents-and-failures.md, market/001-aaru.md, product/087-trust-and-explainability.md)

7. **No campaigns, no consultants, no persuasion ranking.** Publish a one-page acceptable-use policy by Oct 20. It bans voter-level targeting, campaign content, named private individuals, real people speaking in first person, and per-variant message ranking. Requests that name a candidate or an election fall back to aggregate-only output above a minimum segment size, and the check fails closed. We log inbound campaign demand without selling to it. API terms also forbid decisions about individuals (hiring, credit, insurance, housing), which keeps us out of EU Annex III and Colorado's high-risk rules. (product/082-icp-political.md, engineering/074-abuse-and-safety.md, business/091-regulation.md)

8. **The labelling layer ships before Oct 27.** Every response and SDK type includes `simulated: true`, a disclosure string, `world_snapshot_id`, the model and a `sources` field with citation strings. Every chart and agent card carries a visible "AI-simulated" label. "Ask why" is labelled as a simulated account, not as the cause. The web footer carries GDELT attribution and Pew's disclaimer. This is EU AI Act Article 50, which already applies to us. (business/091-regulation.md, data/060-data-licensing-legal.md)

9. **Treat prediction markets as a benchmark only.** We never trade, never pool money and never offer "bet on our forecast." Until someone has read the Kalshi Developer Agreement and Polymarket's terms in a browser, we show only the price at commit time as a number with a source link, and we mirror no price series. Manifold data never enters training. (data/051-prediction-market-apis.md, business/091-regulation.md, market/005-prediction-markets-landscape.md)

10. **Spend the legal hours before the first public hash: one data-licensing review and one trademark knockout.** The capped legal budget covers IPUMS and GLOPOP-S, the survey terms, labelling, the acceptable-use policy and the "REAL WORLD" search in classes 9, 35, 36, 41 and 42. If the name fails, we rename before Oct 20, because the track record attaches to the name. Keep the display name in one config. (product/085-naming-trademark.md, data/060-data-licensing-legal.md, business/091-regulation.md)

## What to cut

- **Game studios, policy units and finance as revenue targets before 2027.** Games have no buyer (market/010-game-npc-ai.md), policy pays little and slowly (product/081-icp-policy-research.md), and funds need years of point-in-time history (market/015-alt-data-finance.md). Newsrooms and academics get free access for distribution and credibility, not a price (product/083-icp-games-media.md).
- **Opt-in twins at launch.** They need GDPR special-category consent, deletion and export, and the evidence shows only a modest lift (business/091-regulation.md, science/017-generative-agents.md).
- **Enterprise annual plans, seat pricing, multiple tiers and a sales team.** We have one plan, one meter and founder-led pilots (product/079-pricing-models.md).
- **Paywalled receipts and any "% accuracy" or "parity" claim** (market/003-synthetic-research-vendors.md).
- **The $153bn TAM and "world model" framing in the pitch.** Use a bottom-up count of buyers times ACV (market/007-market-research-tam.md, market/012-funding-landscape-2026.md).
- **Paid data purchases before launch**: a Gallup World Poll licence, X API volume, and GlobalPetrolPrices (data/048-gallup-pew-global.md, data/055-social-media-data.md, data/056-markets-and-prices.md).
- **Copied AGPL/GPL code** (AgentTorch, FLAME GPU, Polis) on the API path (open-source/033-agenttorch.md, open-source/035-classic-abm.md).

## The 3 numbers I will watch

1. **Committed pilot dollars.** This counts signed pilots and LOIs in dollars, not calls taken. Target: $22.5k (3 pilots) by Nov 2. The path to $100k ARR is about 3 pilots converting at $24k a year ($72k) plus about 50 Pro accounts ($29k), and I expect it in Q1 2027, not this month.
2. **Unbilled LLM spend per day,** meaning free tier plus nightly run plus anything not covered by credits, read from the cost ledger. The ceiling is $60 a day, with an alert at $40. If it jumps, a cap is missing.
3. **Brier difference against market price at commit time, with n,** split by region. It is our only sales collateral and our main reputational exposure. Below about 100 resolved questions the copy says "scored live since <date>" and nothing stronger.
