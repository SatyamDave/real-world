# Seed and pre-seed comps for AI simulation, forecasting and agent infra

- Date: 2026-10-11
- Area: business
- Confidence: medium-low. The late-stage comps (Simile, Aaru, Listen Labs) are confirmed from article pages. True seed and pre-seed comps are thin: the shared web-search budget ran out, so I could only open pages by direct URL. Treat the seed section as a partial list.

## Answer

The money in this category is real but sits at the top. Simile hit $2B five months after a $100M Series A, and Aaru took a Series A at a "headline" $1B. Almost nothing in what I could verify is a classic seed round, and the seed rounds I found are small ($7M). Real World should not pitch against Simile's valuation. It should pitch the one thing they cannot copy: a public, hashed, dated track record that starts now, plus YC's standard $500K.

## Findings

**Simile (the benchmark).**
- Series A: $100M, led by Index Ventures, with Hanabi, A*, Bain Capital Ventures and angels including Andrej Karpathy, Fei-Fei Li, Adam D'Angelo, Guillermo Rauch and Scott Belsky. Announced Feb 12, 2026. The company's post describes "the first AI simulation of society" built on agents of real humans. No valuation stated. ([Simile blog](https://www.simile.com/blog/the-simulation-company), [Simile blog index](https://www.simile.com/blog))
- Series B: over $200M at $2B post-money, announced Jul 30, 2026. Greenoaks and Index co-led. Others: Hanabi, Bain Capital Ventures, A*, Factory, CVS Health Ventures, Definition. Simile says revenue grew 5x in five months. Named customers: CVS Health, Wealthfront, Deloitte, Gallup. ([Series B post](https://www.simile.com/blog/series-b), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/synthetic-user-startup-simile-raises-200m-at-2b-valuation-5-months-after-100m-series-a/))
- No seed round appears on Simile's site. Its public history starts at the $100M.
- Revenue is roughly a third of Listen Labs' ~$30M annualized, per the Listen Labs article below. So $2B is priced on the team (Joon Park's lineage) and growth, not on revenue.

**Aaru.**
- Series A reported Dec 5, 2025, led by Redpoint. Amount undisclosed, one source said above $50M. It was a tiered round: some equity at $1B, lower tiers for other investors, so the blended valuation was under $1B. ARR was below $10M. Founded March 2024. TechCrunch ties its polling work to a correct 2024 New York Democratic primary call. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/05/ai-synthetic-research-startup-aaru-raised-a-series-a-at-a-1b-headline-valuation/))
- Lesson: headline valuations in this category are partly cosmetic. Do not quote $1B as a clean comp.

**Listen Labs (adjacent: AI-run customer research).**
- Series B: $69M, late January 2026, led by Ribbit, at $500M, with Sequoia, Conviction and Pear VC returning. A signed $125M Series C at $1.5B (Menlo to lead) was abandoned in Sept 2026 amid reported Salesforce talks near $2B. About $30M annualized revenue. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/09/ai-research-startup-listen-labs-scrubbed-a-1-5b-funding-round-for-salesforce-talks/))

**Mantic (forecasting).**
- London, founded 2024. Latest round $25M, $29M total raised. Investors: Radical Ventures, Balderton, Thinking Machines, DRW, FT Ventures, M12, Episode 1, angels Charlie Songhurst and Thomas Wolf. Placed 4th of 539 humans in the Metaculus Cup Summer 2025. I could not find the round date or lead. ([Mantic](https://www.mantic.com))
- This is the closest forecasting comp. Its pitch is a measured rank against humans, not a valuation.

**Gudea (narrative-spread prediction, seed).**
- $7M seed, Oct 8, 2026, co-led by Mudita Venture Partners and another firm. Crunchbase's page names Collide Capital in the body, while its listing snippet named Silicon Road Ventures, so the second lead is unconfirmed. About $15M raised since 2023, 25 staff, 39 customers, revenue up 357% year over year. No valuation given. ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/gudea-lands-funding-predict-viral-online-narratives/))
- This is the only real seed comp I verified. It had customers and revenue first.

**YC terms.** $500K total: $125K on a post-money SAFE for 7%, plus $375K on an uncapped MFN SAFE. Pro rata rights. No fees. ([YC deal](https://www.ycombinator.com/deal))

**Investor map from the pages above.** Index, Greenoaks, Redpoint, Bain Capital Ventures, Hanabi, A* (simulation); Ribbit, Sequoia, Menlo, Conviction, Pear (research tooling); Radical, Balderton, DRW, M12 (forecasting); Mudita (early narrative prediction).

## What it means for Real World

- For the product: Simile and Aaru sell private simulations to enterprises. Nobody in this list publishes a hashed daily forecast scored against market prices. That is the visible difference, and it should be the first thing on the receipts page.
- For the engineering: The track record is the asset, so the hash-and-publish pipeline must run from day one, not after launch. Every day it is missing is a day that can never be backfilled.
- For the business: A pre-seed from YC plus angels is the realistic frame. Simile's angels (Karpathy, Li, D'Angelo) show that named technical angels matter in this category. Revenue is not required to raise a seed in 2026, but every comp that raised big had customers (CVS, Gallup) or a measured rank (Metaculus).

## Recommendations

1. In the YC application, cite Simile ($200M at $2B in 5 months) as market proof, and Mantic's Metaculus rank as proof that a measured record beats a story. Do not cite Aaru's $1B without the tier caveat.
2. Do not ask investors for a Simile-like valuation. Plan around the YC SAFE terms ($500K, 7% plus MFN) and a pre-seed cap set after Demo Day interest.
3. Publish the first hashed forecasts before Nov 2. Even 5 weeks of record is a differentiator no funded competitor can fake.
4. Land one named design partner or data partner before the application, ideally a pollster or researcher. Gallup is already Simile's customer, so choose another.
5. Build an angel target list from technical founders who backed Simile and Mantic, and verify who is active in simulation before outreach.
6. Rerun this research with full search access to fill the seed gap (see below).
