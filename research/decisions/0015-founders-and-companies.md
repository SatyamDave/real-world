# 0015: Real World is message testing for startup founders and companies

- Date: 2026-10-13
- Status: accepted (founder decision, Satyam, in chat on 2026-10-13: "cater to the startup founders and companies, make the whole product around that")
- Rests on: [market/007-market-research-tam](../market/007-market-research-tam.md), [market/012-funding-landscape-2026](../market/012-funding-landscape-2026.md), [market/014-who-buys](../market/014-who-buys.md), [market/015-alt-data-finance](../market/015-alt-data-finance.md), [business/092-moat-theory](../business/092-moat-theory.md), [lenses/vc](../lenses/vc.md), [product/080-icp-product-teams](../product/080-icp-product-teams.md), [product/108-consumer-wedge](../product/108-consumer-wedge.md), [0014](0014-ledger-desk.md)

## Decision

The product is built for startup founders and company teams (growth and marketing, pricing and product, comms and PR, market entry): before they ship a launch post, a price change, a headline, an ad line or an announcement, they test it on the simulated public of 16 countries, split by age and education, and see who ignores it, who likes it, who shares it and why. Self-serve and free to start; a paid plan for teams that test often comes once real-model costs are measured. The app's first screen, use cases, copy and the go-to-market speak to them; "Call it" and the public record stay as the proof that the engine is scored in public, secondary on every page. The asset we build is the record of sealed tests marked "Landed" or "Flopped": labelled pairs of what the engine predicted and what really happened. This narrows 0012's audience ("people about to post") to founders and companies; 0014's look, the sealing rules and the three simulation kinds stay.

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creators and anyone about to post (0012) | Largest number of people | The engine models country populations, not a creator's followers, so the answer does not fit the question; no budget behind it ([108](../product/108-consumer-wedge.md)) |
| Founders and companies testing messages (chosen) | Test-a-message is what buyers already pay for out of insights budgets ([014](../market/014-who-buys.md), [007](../market/007-market-research-tam.md)); the category is funded (Simile, Aaru; [VC lens](../lenses/vc.md), [012](../market/012-funding-landscape-2026.md)); the Reaction engine is that product today | Enterprise insights teams expect accuracy evidence we do not have until tests are marked; self-serve founders pay little at first |
| Funds buying a probability feed | Funds buy data | They need point-in-time history we cannot have before 2027 ([015](../market/015-alt-data-finance.md)) |
| Enterprise insights pilots first | Largest checks | Founder-sold pilots with no record; the CRO path stays for later ([lenses/vc](../lenses/vc.md)) |

## Why this one

- Buyers pay for "test this message, price or product against an audience" today, from a market-research line item ([014](../market/014-who-buys.md)); classic research is about $56bn of a $153bn insights industry ([007](../market/007-market-research-tam.md)).
- Money already flows into simulating people for companies ([012](../market/012-funding-landscape-2026.md)), and a seed partner reads the wedge as "a scored simulation API for insights teams" ([lenses/vc](../lenses/vc.md)).
- The only assets nobody can buy later are the public hashed record and outcome data that compounds ([092](../business/092-moat-theory.md)); founders who mark their sealed tests produce exactly that data.

## What would make us revisit it

- After 100 marked tests, the engine's Brier is not below the founders' own gut Brier on the same tests.
- Fewer than 1 in 10 founders who run a test come back within 14 days to mark it.
- Ten founder calls say the 16-country public is not the audience they launch to.
