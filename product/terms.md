# Terms

These terms are a draft that needs legal review. By using Real World you agree to them.

## What Real World is

Real World simulates groups of synthetic people and publishes scored forecasts. Every output is labelled "AI-simulated. Not a poll or a prediction of a real person". Outputs are not advice of any kind: not financial, legal, medical or political. Do not remove the label when you quote, share or republish a result.

## Accounts

An account is free and made with an email address. You are responsible for what happens under your account. You can delete it at any time from settings.

## Acceptable use

Every fork follows the acceptable use rules at `/acceptable-use`. A fork that breaks them is refused.

## API keys

Each API key has a daily dollar cap, and a call that would pass the cap is refused. Keep your key private. You are responsible for calls made with it. We may revoke a key that is used against these terms.

## Public record

Published forecasts and receipts are public and permanent, and are never changed or removed. Every fork link can be opened by anyone who has it.

## Ownership

The Real World code is open source under the Apache License 2.0 ([LICENSE](../LICENSE)). Third-party data keeps its own licence ([data/sources.csv](../data/sources.csv)). You may quote results with their label.

## No warranty

Real World is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. A forecast can be wrong, and the receipts page shows how often it was. We may change, limit or stop the service.
