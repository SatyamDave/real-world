# E006: Assimilation v2 vs persistence on the E003 synthetic world

- Registered: 2026-10-12
- Owner seat: CTO
- Rests on: [assimilation v2 note](../../research/engineering/102-assimilation-v2.md), [decision 0005](../../research/decisions/0005-assimilation-v2.md), [data assimilation for ABMs](../../research/science/022-data-assimilation-abm.md), [data assimilation libraries](../../research/open-source/038-data-assimilation-libs.md), [E003 results](../E003/results/2026-10-12-none/RESULTS.md), [CTO lens](../../research/lenses/cto.md)
- Claim it earns or kills: no public claim (the world is synthetic). It decides whether the daily cycle's default assimilation switches from capped nudging to the v2 Kalman update ([decision 0005](../../research/decisions/0005-assimilation-v2.md)).

## Hypothesis

On the E003 synthetic world, the v2 per-segment Kalman update with an anchor offset has lower day-30 segment belief error than persistence (holding each cell's last poll).

## Primary metric

Root-mean-square error, in logit, between estimated and true segment beliefs over all segment-topic cells on day 30, v2 minus persistence, paired by seed. Negative favours v2.

## Secondary metrics

- Day-30 RMSE, v2 minus capped nudging, and v2 minus no assimilation.
- Day-30 RMSE for cells never observed by day 30, v2 minus no assimilation (tests drift toward or away from the prior where there is no evidence; expected zero).
- Walk misspecification: day-30 RMSE minus persistence for v2 run with its walk SD halved (0.025) and doubled (0.1).
- Calibration of v2's own uncertainty on day 30: RMSE divided by the root of the mean variance P over cells (1 means the filter knows how wrong it is).
- Within-segment spread: the largest absolute change, over segments and topics, of the within-segment SD of v2 beliefs on day 30 against the starting beliefs (v2 shifts whole segments, so it should be zero to rounding).
- Mean RMSE by day for every arm, to show recovery after the shock days.

## Data

Fully synthetic, generated by the E003 code in `engine/rw/experiments.py` with every E003 constant unchanged: a 2,000-agent `population.py` world, its country x age band x education segments, the first 5 topics; the truth is the segment means walking in logit with SD 0.05 per day and shocks of SD 0.5 on days 10 and 20; each day 20% of cells are polled with logit noise of SD 0.2; the model starts from a prior biased by 0.5 plus noise of SD 0.3 per cell. Seeds 1000 to 1049, disjoint from E003's seeds 0 to 49. The v2 design was developed on E003 seeds 0 to 19; those runs are not results and are why new seeds are used. No licence or contamination issue applies.

## Baseline

Persistence, the baseline E003's nudging lost to and the one a skeptic would pick: each cell holds its last poll, and the biased prior until its first. Also capped nudging (the current default) and no assimilation, both exactly as in E003.

v2 settings, fixed now: walk SD 0.05 logit (`KALMAN_WALK_SD_LOGIT`; equal to the generator's walk SD, which is why the misspecification arms exist), prior SD 1.0 logit (`KALMAN_PRIOR_SD_LOGIT`, a judgment), observation SD 0.2 logit (the generator's poll noise, which E003's design states is known). v2 does not know the shock days.

## Sample size and stopping rule

50 seeds per arm, 30 simulated days each, run once. The same size as E003, so the intervals are comparable. No seeds are added after looking.

## Analysis plan

95% interval for the primary metric by bootstrap over seeds (10,000 resamples, seed 7, the E003 procedure). v2 beats persistence only if the whole interval is below zero. Every secondary difference gets the same interval. Report every arm, every seed's rows and every pre-registered metric, including any that make v2 look bad. There are no region or tier splits: the world is synthetic and E003 reported none.

## Kill criterion

If the primary interval is not wholly below zero, the daily default stays capped nudging, we say v2 did not beat persistence, and decision 0005 is revisited before more assimilation work. If v2 wins but either misspecification arm loses to persistence with an interval wholly above zero, the default still switches, and estimating the real walk SD from poll series becomes a blocking item in `company/decisions-needed.md` before any live claim about assimilation.

## Cost cap

No LLM calls and no spend. Compute only, a few minutes on one machine. The runner has no timeout, so the run is stopped by hand if it passes one hour.
