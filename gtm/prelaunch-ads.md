# Pre-launch ads

**Since [0016](../research/decisions/0016-icp-audiences.md):** the product the ads point at is message testing for consumer founders, and creative targets consumer founders, not forecasters or businesses. The allow-list below was written for the earlier record-first page: `hashed` and `public-score` describe the track record and may run only as proof lines beside a message-testing headline, and `your-call` (the daily question) is paused. New headlines are added here and in `landing/src/copy/headlines.ts` in the same PR as the landing copy, each built from a claim in [`messaging.md`](messaging.md).

Paid acquisition for the pre-launch landing page only, Oct 13 to Oct 26. This is a message test and a first-cohort collector, not the launch. The Oct 27 launch channels are unchanged and organic: Show HN points at the live daily page, never at this page or a waitlist ([launch plan](launch-plan.md), [product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md)). Running small paid ads here rests on [product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md) and the CPO lens guidance to keep spend small until cost per signup, message and day-0-to-day-7 numbers exist ([CPO lens](../research/lenses/cpo.md)).

## UTM scheme

Every ad link carries these parameters. The landing page reads `utm_content` to pick the headline and records the chosen `headline_variant` on every analytics event and on the signup row ([landing-page spec](../product/landing-page.md)).

| Parameter | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `utm_source` | `reddit`, `x` | the ad network |
| `utm_medium` | `paid_social` | one value for both networks |
| `utm_campaign` | `prelaunch_waitlist_2026_10` | one campaign for the whole test |
| `utm_content` | a headline id from the allow-list below | exactly the id; an unknown or missing value falls back to the default headline |
| `utm_term` | a short audience slug, lower-case with underscores (for example `consumer_founders`, `indie_makers`) | optional; for reporting only, never shown on the page |

Example: `?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=prelaunch_waitlist_2026_10&utm_content=receipts&utm_term=forecasting`.

## Headline allow-list

Each headline is built only from a claim on the "Claims we may make" list in [`messaging.md`](messaging.md). No other headline may run. Ids match [`product/landing-page.md`](../product/landing-page.md) and `landing/src/copy/headlines.ts`.

| id | Headline | Rests on the allowed claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `receipts` (default) | "Simulations with receipts." | the short-form one-liner in [messaging](messaging.md) |
| `hashed` | "Forecasts, hashed before the outcome." | "Forecasts hashed before the outcome, scored in public since <date>" ([messaging](messaging.md)) |
| `public-score` | "We publish our misses." | "A record that cannot be backfilled" and scoring in public, misses included ([messaging](messaging.md)) |
| `your-call` | "Make your call before the world does." | the daily-question forecast hook, which previews the core loop without showing any simulated number ([product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md), [CPO lens](../research/lenses/cpo.md)) |

Forbidden in any creative, per [`messaging.md`](messaging.md): no accuracy percentage, no "beats the market" or any ranking claim, no "world model", no "100k agents" headline, no "whole world" without tiers, no fabricated hash or probability.

## Creative rules

- No election content and no policy content in any creative: no candidate, party, office, vote, ballot, or named political figure, and no economic-policy position. Lead with the product, not a political event ([product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md)). This also keeps the ads clear of the "social issues, elections or politics" review categories on X, whose rules and review times [product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md) marks unverified, and of the founder ruling still open on election questions ([decisions-needed item 2](../company/decisions-needed.md)).
- Every creative carries the "AI-simulated" disclosure ([messaging](messaging.md)).
- The teaser forecast used in creative, if any, is a non-election, non-policy example (the launch teaser is the Fed rate decision of Oct 28, 2026; it appears only in the post-signup Typeform, not in ad creative).
- Ad image sizes are generated by `landing/ads/` and keep text inside each platform's safe zone.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reddit | `reddit` | Oct 13-15 (first 3-day flight), then by the kill rule | CRO | [product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md) |
| X | `x` | Oct 13-15 (first 3-day flight), then by the kill rule | CRO | [product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md), [launch plan](launch-plan.md) (X is an experiment channel) |

## Budget cap

- $50 per day per channel, for at most 3 days per flight. This is a founder-set cap for a message test, not a measured cost; cost per signup is unknown until the ads run and is reported with its n ([product/101](../research/product/101-prelaunch-landing-waitlist-ads.md), [CPO lens](../research/lenses/cpo.md)).
- Total first flight is at most $300 (two channels × $50 × 3 days). No spend beyond the cap without a new line here.

## Kill rule

- A channel runs at most 3 days at $50/day. Pause a channel at the end of any day on which it spent the full $50 and produced zero waitlist signups.
- After the first flight, report cost per signup by channel and by `headline_variant`, each with its n, before any further spend ([CPO lens](../research/lenses/cpo.md)). Retire any headline with zero signups after the cap is spent; keep spending only on headlines whose cost per signup the founder approves.
- Stop all spend immediately if any creative is flagged by a platform for the elections/politics category, and record what was flagged before changing it.
