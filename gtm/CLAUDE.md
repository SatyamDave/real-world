# gtm/

Go-to-market: who uses Real World first, at what price, how we launch, and what we may say. Since [0012](../research/decisions/0012-will-it-land-and-call-it.md) everything here sells two consumer jobs, "Will this land?" and "Call it"; B2B material is marked paused, never silently deleted. Everything here is public-facing in the end, so an overclaim here becomes a public miss.

## A great contribution

- Every fact about a market, competitor, price, channel or customer links a research note by repo path, or a committed artifact.
- Recommendations that come from a lens say which lens, and where lenses disagree the doc says so and points at `company/decisions-needed.md`.
- Price points and caps are stated with the cost they rest on and whether that cost is measured or estimated.
- Customer evidence is named and dated: which call, which buyer type, what they said. Until calls happen, write "no calls yet".
- A change to an allowed or forbidden claim in `messaging.md` goes through a decision record.
- Launch steps have a date, an owner seat and the note or lens they come from.
- Copy uses one word per thing from [product/glossary.md](../product/glossary.md); `messaging.md` is the voice every surface copies, so a synonym here spreads.

## What gets rejected

- Any claim on the forbidden list in `messaging.md`, anywhere in this folder, including drafts.
- Invented customers, quotes, logos, traffic numbers, conversion rates or market sizes.
- Top-down market sizing used as a forecast of our revenue.
- Channels, tiers, or segments the lenses cut, added without a decision record.
- Asking anyone to upvote, or any tactic the channel's rules forbid.
- Copy that hides the coverage tier, the spread, or the n behind a score.
